About

I'm a biotechnology graduate from Sambalpur, Odisha. My master's thesis looked at the anti-arthritic and antioxidant potential of Ailanthus excelsa. I prepared and standardised the extracts, ran DPPH and FRAP assays, and carried out in vitro and in vivo anti-inflammatory studies.

Before that I trained in biochemistry at Centurion University. There I identified 84 WRKY transcription factor genes in Dichanthelium oligosanthes, and studied odorant-binding protein expression in the rice moth Corcyra cephalonica using RT-PCR.

I'm looking for a PhD or research associate position where I can combine wet-lab work with computational analysis.

Genome-wide analysis of WRKY transcription factors in Dichanthelium oligosanthes

Bachelor's thesis, Centurion University of Technology and Management

  • Retrieved genomic data from NCBI and identified 84 WRKY genes (DoWRKY1–DoWRKY84) with BLAST and HMM profiling.
  • Ran alignment (MEGA, CLUSTAL W), phylogenetic and motif analysis (MEME), gene structure (GSDS), GO annotation (DeepGO) and localisation prediction (mGOASVM).
